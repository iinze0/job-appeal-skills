# CI/CD Review

Evaluate automation quality and safety.

## Check
- Secret handling and least-privilege tokens
- Sources of flakiness
- Caching and speed opportunities
- Permission scope of jobs
- Failure visibility and notifications
- Deployment safety (gates, rollbacks)
